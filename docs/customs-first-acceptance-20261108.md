## Changes

- Shared customs-first role preserves transport/other services and digital identity.
- Optional customs intake context survives corrections and is archived with a new request.
- Dated official sources selected locally by topic provide procedural documents, special regimes, valuation/origin, post-clearance and sector-specific guidance. Selection supplies context, not a case decision or evidence of live verification.
- Current June 2026 amendments take precedence over conflicting older guides. Source conflicts, consultation drafts and unverified cases are explicit.
- Actual public SABER lookup remains bounded and deterministic for certificate evidence. Tariff lookup remains a cached GCC schedule with dated caveat and required lookup on explicit questions; model synthesis is not deterministic validation of each duty or semantic classification.
- FASAH AI restricts codes/rates/alternatives to each original item, rejects invalid indices/truncated/oversize output and exposes partial coverage. Missing/partial/unresolved classification blocks complete assisted entry. Analysis is bound to source fingerprints; changed or unbound legacy sources block entry and individual copy controls. All original review, login and submission controls remain.
- Current SFDA/country/establishment distinctions and verified training-topic corrections are included. No noisy ASR text, unsupported fees/percentages, universal pallet fine or promised storage waiver is imported.

- Shipment fee checklist separates our approved prices from carrier/terminal/regulatory/appointment charges. Units, payer, source date, VAT, inclusions, deposits, prepaid amounts and duplicated pass-through charges are explicit. Dated external amounts are not a shipment quote; current appointment amounts and complete port-storage tariffs are not verified here.
- Non-food SFDA, medicine/device/cosmetic, feed/wood and chemical/telecom routing is scoped and retains unresolved sectors instead of declaring completeness. Source document dates are not treated as effective dates automatically.
- Explicit SABER fee questions retain deterministic certificate evidence plus a separately dated fee note. Mixed transport-price questions explicitly defer the unpriced transport portion, rather than assigning a SABER fee to transport.

## Verification boundaries

They do not establish live-model Arabic answer quality or certify every video sentence. Source review is dated, not a new live connector to every authority.

Before activation run required PostgreSQL/runner acceptance and exact-head CI; separately evaluate scoped synthetic staging conversations with a real model if authorized. Browser QA of the updated coverage UI is distinct from HTML unit assertions. No user data is needed for these acceptance cases.
