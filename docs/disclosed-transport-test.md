# Disclosed transport test

New captures submitted through **Save for test** (`/naqliat/manual`, `capture_method=manual_test`) are persistently marked as tests. Their fingerprint is separate from an identical real capture. Existing records are not reclassified or modified by this migration.

The initial owner inquiry asks only price, payment method and whether loading is inside or outside the port. It has no visible shipment reference. Test inquiries prepend the exact disclosure. References remain internal; a verified quoted reply is matched to the stored provider receipt, otherwise exactly one pending owner shipment or one unique route may be selected. Multiple or stale/conflicting references and unknown quotes fail closed with natural route clarification; no newest-row fallback or replay of earlier ambiguous terms is allowed. The locked workflow repeats this correlation. Port status is stored only from explicit, unambiguous text; weight and unloading are never invented, and existing driver-offer completeness checks remain.

The owner may also be the administrator; administrator commands remain commands. Test creation does not send an owner inquiry automatically. The detail page previews the proposed text only before an inquiry has a provider receipt, so a wording update does not display a new text as an already sent message. Its dedicated button still requires admin role, CSRF, explicit preview approval, send_whatsapp permission and recent MFA. Existing receipts and sent records are not rewritten or resent. Complete owner terms prepare a draft without automatically broadcasting.

Outside a verified active WhatsApp conversation window, the new wording requires a separately approved exact template: `afaaq_transport_owner_inquiry_v3_ar` for normal inquiries and `afaaq_transport_test_owner_inquiry_v1_ar` for disclosed tests. Both payloads are prepared in `required_templates()` for the existing guarded template-registration UI. Code publication does not register a template or imply provider approval. The original owner v2 and driver v1 payloads are preserved, and exact approved-template matching remains fail-closed. No alternative old wording is silently sent when the new template is pending.

The quote fields follow [Zernio's OpenAPI](https://docs.zernio.com/api/openapi): top-level `metadata.quotedMessage.platformMessageId`, falling back to `metadata.quotedMessageId` only when the resolved platform ID is absent. The internal Zernio message ID and current inbound event/message ID are not a quote receipt.

The driver preview prominently uses **اختبار للنظام فقط، لا توجد حمولة فعلية ولا حاجة للتحرك** inside the approved template's first reference parameter, including the repeated acceptance instruction. An administrator must review and explicitly approve the test, exact message and displayed recipient list. Existing role, CSRF, MFA and outbound controls remain in force. Changed disclosure or audience invalidates approval. Direct delivery cannot bypass approval, and manual-send links are suppressed in test previews.

Only a registered, actually sent recipient with a provider receipt may reply with the exact disclosed acceptance instruction. Administrator identity never becomes a driver identity. The first valid test reply records `test_completed`; it does not assign an operational driver, create shipment revenue/cost, or enter business-shipment KPIs. A provider receipt means acceptance of the request, not proof of delivery or reading. Uncertain sends are not retried automatically.

Tests use synthetic identities and mocked providers. `tests/transport_postgres_acceptance.py` runs additional isolated PostgreSQL checks in `transport_disclosed_acceptance.py`, including signed owner/admin correlation, duplicate/racing approval and delivery, receipt handling, and unchanged real records. This change alone sends no test messages and authorizes no production test.

## Unsent driver preview validation

Driver-contact validation retains ordinary international E.164 handling and rejects Saudi contact numbers containing domestic `0` after `+966` or a national part other than nine digits. This is an ordinary driver-contact rule, not a claim about every special Saudi service-number range. Source: CST [National Numbering Plan](https://www.cst.gov.sa/-/media/cst-website-app/data/media/files/National-Numbering-Plan.ashx), mobile numbering and international dialing sections. Numbers are never repaired or written back to driver records.

Existing wholly unattempted disclosed-test drafts show a proposed updated preview at `/commands/broadcast/{id}`. The explicit refresh checkbox/button calls the admin/CSRF/MFA-guarded `/refresh-preview` action. It locks the draft, checks the exact proposed snapshot, preserves the original audience, keeps excluded rows with a reason, records an audit event and clears prior approval. Any receipt, attempted recipient status, confirmation/completion/acceptance or reply evidence prevents refresh. It does not send. A separate fresh broadcast approval is still required. Delivery revalidates the eligible audience before crossing the provider boundary; invalid contacts are not counted as API delivery failures.

The stored `loading_port_status` qualifies only the existing origin template variable, e.g. `جدة (التحميل داخل الميناء)`. The stored city, fixed driver template text, explicit test disclaimer and repeated acceptance instruction are unchanged. Excluded rows remain excluded after a successful test/real acceptance and never enter the approval audience digest.

## Read diagnostics and transport batch limits

A transport batch reuses short-lived, account-scoped validation/template/conversation-list reads while preserving uncached exact-recipient incoming-message checks for the WhatsApp window. GET-only transient retries are bounded by attempts, per-read wait budget and total batch wait budget. Rate headers and Retry-After are honored; no sending POST is retried automatically. Auth failures fail closed without a retry loop. No credentials, authorization headers, provider response bodies or raw exception text enter the diagnostic record.

Recipient evidence now distinguishes preflight failure, the committed pre-POST boundary, provider acceptance and an uncertain POST outcome. The final boundary rechecks campaign/recipient state after any wait, so a completed test cannot initiate another message. Provider acceptance remains distinct from delivery/read confirmation; later delivery failures never make a receipt-bearing recipient eligible for automatic replay. Existing receipt rows are not reclassified or reset.

This change adds no retry/recovery UI and performs no historical resend. The existing send route still accepts only an unattempted draft. Read-only preflight diagnostics do not resolve an account's separate business/payment eligibility restriction, which must be reviewed through the provider's supported account flow.

## Explicit linked recovery of a failed disclosed test

`/commands/broadcast/{id}/recovery` adds one bounded recovery attempt for an already failed, explicitly approved test. It does not reset or rewrite original recipient rows, receipt IDs, counters, errors or timestamps. Preparation only reads Zernio and appends a linked batch and attempt records. The send action separately binds approval to the exact stored message, eligible audience, sender account and source outcome snapshot. Both actions retain admin, CSRF, send_whatsapp and recent MFA checks. Existing campaign and shipment identity continue to correlate signed inbound replies.

Eligibility is fail-closed: an original `failed` row must have proven preflight evidence and no receipt, sent/reply timestamp, POST boundary or provider response status. The exact legacy error accepted here is emitted exclusively by `read()` before POST in the verified deployed source `96ac945c6bda1dc7df868e50fa7e318f418e3975`; other generic errors are not proof. Receipt-bearing originals require an exact account, conversation, recipient and message match with current `deliveryStatus=failed` and no delivered/read timestamp. A fresh uncached failure check is repeated at the final dispatch guard. Missing, mismatched, sent, delivered, read, deleted and uncertain evidence cannot authorize a resend.

Evidence and receipt contracts follow [Zernio's OpenAPI](https://docs.zernio.com/api/openapi), `GET /v1/inbox/conversations` and `GET /v1/inbox/conversations/{conversationId}/messages`; these reads do not mark a conversation read. Original and new provider message IDs remain separate. Recovery reconciliation appends delivery observations instead of overwriting send outcomes. Accepted means the provider returned an ID, not delivery or reading.

The bounded flow supports at most 81 existing SA/UAE recipients and a total 25 SAR ceiling. Published [Zernio October 2026 pricing](https://zernio.com/whatsapp-api-pricing) lists both markets at $0.0576 per delivered marketing message, plus at most $0.0001 sent-message meter. A 0.30 SAR reservation per new attempt conservatively covers these fees at 3.75 SAR/USD plus 15% tax. Previously failed provider receipts retain a 0.01 SAR meter reserve, while any original outcome not proven preflight or provider-failed retains a full 0.30 SAR reserve. The 66 preflight + 15 provider-failed case reserves 24.45 SAR including those prior meters. Uncertain reservations are never reclaimed. Unsupported destinations, changed source/audience/account, excess budget and rates after 2026 Q4 fail closed. This is a conservative control, not an asserted billing receipt or new subscription.

Each original recipient has at most one recovery record and each attempt can cross the POST boundary once. Repeated forms and concurrent workers cannot resend a claimed attempt. An uncertain outcome remains unavailable for replay. First exact, signed, genuine TEST acceptance requires a new accepted receipt when a retry exists and the same sender account; it completes the existing test without modifying its original send records, assigning operational work or creating revenue/cost. Parent-campaign locks stop later sends and retain the first winner. No recovery preparation or code deployment itself sends messages.
