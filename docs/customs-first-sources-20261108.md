# Source register and superseded guidance

Verified review date: 8 November 2026. The current detailed register is customs-current-sources-20261108.md. This candidate supplies dated reference knowledge and does not claim that every rule, sector or shipment has been checked live.

## Source precedence

Use the current consolidated ZATCA procedure rules and their June 2026 amendment over conflicting older explanatory guides. Use the actual goods, regime, mode, port, origin and authorization to decide applicability. A web-page update date or dated PDF directory is not, by itself, a legal effective date. Draft consultations are not enacted law.

## Explicitly retired assumptions

- A general 48-hour statement is not the deadline for every party and transport mode. Current marine advance-filing rules distinguish carrier obligations and importer filing after manifest notice.
- The older land-transit manifest/invoice-if-available archive list is superseded by the current source-country customs declaration and documents requested as needed. RFS/TIR require their own route conditions.
- An old re-export example without a prior import declaration does not override current Article 31 linkage and identification requirements.
- Old refund or guarantee deadlines must not be used to compute current eligibility without verifying their route and current validity.
- The prior SFDA food guide does not override the current food guide and country/product/establishment lists. A factory halal certificate is not interchangeable with shipment certificates.
- Old mandatory-pallet announcements must not override Mawani circular 13/2026 postponing the container pallet requirement until further notice. This is not a blanket packaging exemption.
- The training video is not a legal or price authority. Unclear transcription, unverified percentages, rates, free-day examples, waiver promises and ambiguous certificate terms were not imported.
