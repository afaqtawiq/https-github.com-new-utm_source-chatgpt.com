# SABER knowledge review, 7 November 2026

## Confirmed official guidance

- SASO's announcement dated 26 August 2025 takes effect on 1 October 2025. Shipment certification must precede the import customs declaration, for both regulated and nonregulated products. Regulated products follow product-conformity certification then shipment certification; nonregulated products use self-declaration then shipment certification. The announcement directs users to search SABER by HS code or product keywords.
  https://www.saso.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/news/Pages/saso-news-1483.aspx
- SABER's nonregulated-products page expressly requires product registration, self-declaration and a shipment certificate. Nonregulated does not mean exempt from SABER.
  https://saber.sa/Home/NonRegulatedProducts
- SABER's HS lookup supplies the product description, technical regulation, required certificates and additional requirements. The site says it updates the list regularly. The exact national code and actual product description matter; neither a four-digit heading nor a tariff duty result is a verified conformity status.
  https://saber.sa/Home/HSCodes
- The non-consumer-display route provides a conditional exemption from the product certificate, not a blanket exemption from shipment certification. The official terms list separate documentary requirements for individuals, manufacturing, samples, government projects and other cases.
  https://saber.sa/Home/Terms
  https://saber.sa/home/HelpCenter

## Coal and charcoal: verification limit

No opened official source established a blanket SABER exemption for all coal or charcoal. Do not turn that lack of verification into either an exemption or a definitive positive certificate classification for a specific charcoal line.

Wood/vegetable and shell/nut charcoal must not be conflated with mineral coal. ZATCA's chapter 44 is wood/articles of wood/wood charcoal, whereas its heading 2701 includes mineral coal and coal fuels. The appropriate detailed code depends on the product, composition and use. No national charcoal code was confirmed as a SABER exemption in this review.

- ZATCA chapter 44: https://eservices.zatca.gov.sa/sites/sc/ar/SCTariffs/Pages/Pages/chaptersPage.aspx?SectionNo=9
- ZATCA mineral-coal rows: https://eservices.zatca.gov.sa/sites/sc/ar/SCTariffs/Pages/Pages/ItemTreePage%20.aspx?HarmonizedNumber=270119000003&IsDlg=1
- Separate environmental requirements exist for firewood/charcoal activities: official executive regulation, article 4, lists import, gathering, transport, storage, sale, promotion and certain wood-waste preparation as permit/licence activities. This is not proof of SABER exemption.
  https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=17829

## Caveats and implementation boundaries

- Some search-index snippets for paginated SABER URLs showed coal rows while opening the same URL returned different current rows. Those snippets were not accepted as proof of a current coal conformity classification.
- The SABER exact-code query for 440220000000 returned no results. Other parameterized requests were inaccessible through the research tool. A missing result is not evidence of exemption or a verified correct national code.
- An older official FAQ says industrial-exemption raw materials do not currently require platform registration. The current non-consumer-display route and newer shipment guidance require case-specific review; the old FAQ was not turned into a blanket exemption. Historical FAQ: https://product-safety.saber.sa/Home/FAQs
- The application presently has a tariff lookup, not a live official SABER-status connector. This change does not invent one or claim one exists. It adds dated examples, distinctions and the official lookup reference, and requires verification for an individual shipment.
- It does not change prices, permissions, account settings, document access, certificate submission or external communications. Tests use a mocked provider and prove prompt wiring, not real-model factual reliability. No deployment is included.
